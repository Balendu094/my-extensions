Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

ColorPro does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device only.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or telemetry of any kind. No data is ever sent to any server.

Local Storage Only

Color history and saved palettes are stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data never leaves your browser and is never shared with anyone.

Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — Activate color picker on current tab only
  • scripting — Inject the color picker tool into the page
  • storage — Save your color history locally on device
  • clipboardWrite — Copy picked color to your clipboard

Third Parties

ColorPro does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking scripts.
